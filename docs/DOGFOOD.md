# Dogfooding Log

Use Actions daily to test the product rules in [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md). Record friction and confusing or judgmental language; do not treat missed use as failure.

## What to check

- Can I reach a useful action quickly?
- Does completing, editing, pausing, or resting feel clear and reversible where expected?
- Does the app preserve a calm, non-punitive tone after an inactive period?
- Did anything feel confusing, broken, or slower than it should be?
